About SmartCalc Tools

Who We Are

SmartCalc Tools is a collection of free, fast, and accurate online calculators designed to help you make informed decisions about your finances, health, and daily life. We are operated by SJA Studio, a small team passionate about making complex calculations simple and accessible.

Our Mission

We believe everyone deserves access to professional-grade calculation tools without cost or complexity. Whether you're calculating your mortgage payments, estimating your tax obligations, checking your BMI, or converting units, our tools provide instant, reliable results.

What We Offer

  • Financial Calculators: Mortgage, compound interest, loan, salary, and retirement calculators
  • Tax Calculators: Gift tax, transfer tax, VAT, freelancer tax, and income tax tools
  • Health Tools: BMI calculator, calorie calculator
  • Legal Calculators: Car accident settlement, workers' compensation estimators
  • Utility Tools: Unit converters, JSON formatter, word counter, and more

Privacy & Security

All calculations run entirely in your browser. We never collect, store, or transmit any data you enter into our tools. Your privacy is our priority. See our Privacy Policy for details.
