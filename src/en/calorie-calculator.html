What is TDEE?

TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) is the total number of calories you burn each day. It's calculated by multiplying your BMR by an activity factor.

Mifflin-St Jeor Formula

  • Men: BMR = 10 × weight(kg) + 6.25 × height(cm) - 5 × age + 5
  • Women: BMR = 10 × weight(kg) + 6.25 × height(cm) - 5 × age - 161

Weight Loss Tips

A deficit of 500 calories per day results in approximately 1 pound (0.45 kg) of weight loss per week. Never eat below your BMR for extended periods.
