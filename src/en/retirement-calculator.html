The 4% Rule

The 4% rule suggests you can withdraw 4% of your retirement savings annually without running out of money for 30 years. Multiply your desired annual income by 25 to find your target nest egg.

Start Early

Thanks to compound interest, starting 10 years earlier can nearly double your retirement savings, even with the same monthly contribution.
