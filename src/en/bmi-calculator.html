What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a screening tool that estimates body fat based on height and weight. While it doesn't measure body fat directly, it correlates with more direct measures of body fat.

BMI Categories (WHO)

  • Underweight: Below 18.5
  • Normal weight: 18.5 – 24.9
  • Overweight: 25.0 – 29.9
  • Obesity Class I: 30.0 – 34.9
  • Obesity Class II: 35.0 – 39.9
  • Obesity Class III: 40.0 and above

Limitations

BMI doesn't distinguish between muscle and fat mass. Athletes and muscular individuals may have a high BMI without excess body fat. Always consult a healthcare provider for a complete health assessment.
