How to Use This Mortgage Calculator

Enter your home price, down payment, interest rate, and loan term to calculate your estimated monthly mortgage payment. The calculator includes property tax and home insurance for a complete picture of your monthly housing costs.

Understanding Your Mortgage Payment

  • Principal & Interest (P&I): The core mortgage payment that goes toward paying off your loan balance and interest charges.
  • Property Tax: Annual property tax divided by 12 months, typically 0.5% to 2.5% of home value depending on location.
  • Home Insurance: Annual homeowner's insurance premium divided by 12 months.

30-Year vs 15-Year Mortgage

A 30-year mortgage has lower monthly payments but higher total interest. A 15-year mortgage has higher monthly payments but saves tens of thousands in interest over the life of the loan. Use this calculator to compare both options.

2026 Mortgage Rates

As of August 2026, average 30-year fixed mortgage rates range from 5.5% to 7.0%, while 15-year fixed rates range from 5.0% to 6.5%. Rates vary by lender, credit score, and loan amount.
