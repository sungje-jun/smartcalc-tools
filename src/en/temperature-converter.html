Conversion Formulas

  • °F = °C × 9/5 + 32
  • °C = (°F - 32) × 5/9
  • K = °C + 273.15

Common Temperatures

  • Water freezes: 0°C = 32°F = 273.15K
  • Water boils: 100°C = 212°F = 373.15K
  • Body temperature: 37°C = 98.6°F
